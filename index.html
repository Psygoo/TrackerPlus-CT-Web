What do these conditions mean?
1:2–8:8
Play on a specific pass of a repeating cycle.
PR / !PR
Follow or invert the previous relevant condition on the same track.
NE / !NE
Follow or invert the last conditional trigger on the neighbouring track to the left.
1S / !1S
Play only on the first pass, or on all later passes.
